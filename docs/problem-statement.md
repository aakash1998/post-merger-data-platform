# Problem Statement

Rocky Mountain Retail Group has acquired Stampede City Commerce.

Both companies use different operational systems, database technologies, schemas, identifiers, data formats, and refresh patterns. Leadership cannot obtain one reliable view of customers, sales, products, inventory, finance, and operations.

The goal is to build a production-style unified enterprise data platform that integrates both companies without requiring the immediate replacement of their operational applications.

## One-line statement

**Build a unified enterprise data platform for two companies after an acquisition.**
